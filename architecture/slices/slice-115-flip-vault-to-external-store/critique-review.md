## Missed findings

- **M-add-1 (Major): the B2 fix's `OP_OUT_OF_SCOPE` reclassification weakens the gate's coverage floor — the design never re-pins `_OP_CLASS_FLOOR[OP_OUT_OF_SCOPE]`.** Attacking the fix per AP-2: moving 11 active-folder ops out of `OP_DEFERRED_TO_FLIP` into `OP_OUT_OF_SCOPE` means the existing `_OP_CLASS_FLOOR[OP_OUT_OF_SCOPE]=23` (L639) should rise to ~34 to keep the anti-silent-shrink guarantee AP-12 demands — otherwise a future slice can silently drop a genuinely-contended write into `OP_OUT_OF_SCOPE` (now carrying 11 ops of slack) and the floor never reds. The design specifies the `11→0` DEFERRED re-pin + the wiring but says nothing about re-pinning the OUT_OF_SCOPE floor to absorb the 11 it receives. A reclassification that moves ops between gate-visible buckets must re-pin BOTH floors. Proposed fix: re-pin `_OP_CLASS_FLOOR[OP_OUT_OF_SCOPE]` to the new post-reclassification count + a non-vacuity test that a 1-op silent demotion into OUT_OF_SCOPE reds. Also: confirm the reclass is keyed on the SINK (`_ACTIVE_FOLDER_RE.search(sink)`, L679), ABOVE the in-loop-skill check (L681) — else in-loop active-folder writes fall through to the allowlist/`OP_UNROUTED` path.

- **M-add-2 (Major): EOL/gitattributes hazard on the move — the external store is a plain non-git directory, so `.gitattributes eol=lf` does NOT govern it, and only 1 of 1074 vault `.md` files is EOL-pinned in-tree.** The first Critic checked "no CRLF/EOL churn" as a byte-faithfulness property of the copy but missed the asymmetry: `.gitattributes` pins `eol=lf` for exactly one vault file — `architecture/slice-queue.md` (slice-109/ADR-098, for the CAS byte-identity precondition). The other ~1073 `architecture/**/*.md` are NOT eol-pinned, so under `core.autocrlf` (documented Windows repo; EOL-DRIFT-1 burned slice-033, recurred 094/097/109) the in-tree working copy read during the migrate may already carry CRLF. A byte-faithful copy then carries CRLF INTO the external store, where no renormalization will ever touch it (plain dir). The B3 manifest hash PASSES (bytes match) while the external store diverges from the LF canonical the EOL-agnostic comparators assume. AND the slice-queue.md `eol=lf` CAS byte-identity precondition (ADR-098 AC4) loses its home post-flip. Proposed fix: either (a) the migrate normalizes `.md`/text to canonical LF on write (and the manifest verify compares LF-normalized content), with the external slice-queue.md being LF re-satisfying ADR-098 AC4; OR (b) ADR-107 explicitly records the external store inherits in-tree EOL + the EOL-agnostic comparators are the durable layer AND gives the CAS byte-identity precondition a post-flip home. Held at Major until the Builder states where ADR-098's precondition lives post-flip.

- **m-add-3 (Minor): AC1 "full suite passes against the flipped store" is under-specified — the suite is location-AGNOSTIC (each test pins its own fixture), it is not actually run against the live external store.** `tests/_vault_isolation.py` (slice-110/ADR-101) pins each vault-resolving test to its OWN tmp fixture, explicitly because the consumer-freeze cascade (`tools/_vault_paths.py` L32-43: env+config read ONCE at import) means in-process `pytest` CANNOT see a config-driven external `VAULT_ROOT`. So plain `pytest` post-flip proves "location-agnostic" (the real safety property), NOT "green against the external store." The verification-plan `python -c "...VAULT_ROOT"` DOES prove external resolution at process level, but the "full pytest green" line conflates the two. Proposed fix: AC1 states explicitly that suite-green is the location-AGNOSTIC contract (per-test fixture isolation) and live-external resolution is verified separately via process-level `VAULT_ROOT`/`vault_is_external` + the Charter live-fire writes. Low severity (underlying safety IS covered; only the WORDING is defective).

## Severity adjustments

- **M3 (concurrent slice in-flight) — SEVERITY-WRONG on the FIX: the finding is correctly a Major, but its accepted fix is incomplete in a way that re-opens a data-loss path.** The first Critic correctly identified the sibling-branch-re-introduces-tracked-`architecture/` hazard and recommended `git branch --list 'slice/*'` minus self == ∅ (design.md step 0). But that guard is blind to the `BRANCHLESS_IN_FLIGHT` class `tools/stranded_slice_audit.py` (L381-434, slice-092/ADR-084/R-31) was built to distinguish — a scaffolded `architecture/slices/slice-NNN/` folder with a non-terminal milestone and NO `slice/*` ref. A branchless in-flight slice's active folder is in-tree, **untracked**, under `architecture/`; at the flip's `git rm -r --cached architecture/` + physical-remove (step 5) it is **destroyed**, not migrated (the full-branch-tree migration enumerates tracked content only). The design even cites `stranded_slice_audit` as evidence the case is "currently satisfiable" — yet specifies a WEAKER guard (`git branch --list`) than the audit it cites. Recommend the quiesce guard be `tools/stranded_slice_audit --json` returns no `BRANCHLESS_IN_FLIGHT` AND no live `slice/*` (minus self), not bare `git branch --list`. Filed SEVERITY-WRONG (fix incompleteness, data-loss path) rather than a new Blocker because M3's intent and Major severity are right.
