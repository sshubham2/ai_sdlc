- **Claim under review**: design.md L57 — "This pre-empts a meta-meta-recursive build-time fire: if THIS canonical body in `agents/critique.md` contained those literals, the body itself would trigger BC-PROJ-2 at every BC-1 audit run on `agents/critique.md`." design.md L210 — "The NEW canonical body in `agents/critique.md` stays BC-1-clean." design.md L57-L58 use this framing as the rationale for choosing abstract over literal anchor descriptions.
- **Issue**: Per `tools/build_checks_audit.py::_read_slice_text` (L435-L442), BC-1 reads ONLY `mission-brief.md` + `design.md` of the slice being audited as the haystack for keyword/anchor matching. BC-1 does NOT audit the content of `agents/critique.md` directly. The keyword-path applicability check operates on `slice_text = mission-brief + design.md`, never on changed-file content. Therefore: literals in the canonical body of `agents/critique.md` would NOT cause BC-1 to fire "on `agents/critique.md`" in any subsequent /build-slice run. The actual mechanism by which `agents/critique.md` interacts with BC-1 is the GLOB path (`Applies to: agents/**/*.md` in BC-PROJ-1), which fires when a future slice modifies `agents/critique.md` and so includes it in `--changed-files` — but at that point the negative-anchor filter operates on the FUTURE slice's mission-brief+design, not on `agents/critique.md`'s body. The design's rationale "abstract framing keeps the canonical body BC-1-clean" is therefore based on a misreading of BC-1 semantics.
- **Evidence**:
  - `tools/build_checks_audit.py::_read_slice_text` L435-L442 — explicitly reads `mission-brief.md` + `design.md`, returns concatenation as `slice_text`
  - `tools/build_checks_audit.py::_rule_applies` L385-L432 — `slice_text` is the keyword/anchor haystack, never changed-file content
  - design.md L57-L58, L210 — the false-premised rationale
- **Proposed fix**: Either (a) keep the abstract framing in the canonical body for a DIFFERENT, correct reason — e.g., "abstract framing reads better at the Critic-prompt level; concrete anchors belong in slice-N reflection records as empirical primary sources, not in the agent prompt's prose" — and remove the misframed BC-1 rationale from design.md L57; OR (b) acknowledge that the choice is stylistic / readability-driven and drop the "meta-meta-recursive build-time fire" framing entirely. Option (a) preserves the abstract-framing decision (which IS reasonable) while correcting the rationale.
- **Builder draft**: ACCEPTED-FIXED at design.md § Notes on canonical-body content (Option (a)). Rationale corrected to stylistic / reusable-artifact-readability framing: canonical Critic-prompt artifact at `agents/critique.md` is consumed by every future Critic invocation; abstract framing of anchor STRINGS reads better there; rule-ID `BC-PROJ-2` named explicitly (matches existing Dim 9 convention); literal anchor strings belong in slice-N reflection records as primary sources.
